**Introduction**
Urban traffic over the recent years has become increasingly complex, which demands the need for smarter and more adaptive intersection control systems. “Proliferation of Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) connectivity in the recent years could be a significant game changer in intersection control.” (Ashtiani et al., 2020). Connected Autonomous vehicles (CAVs) stand out to be transformative as they are capable of exchanging critical information with intersection controllers in order to optimize traffic flow. CAVs can adjust their arrival times which leads to greater intersection efficiency and reduced delays. The widespread adoption of CAVs can possibly lead to the elimination of traditional traffic signals altogether. This report will explore intersection management using Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) that would be used to harness the full potential of CAV coordination.
